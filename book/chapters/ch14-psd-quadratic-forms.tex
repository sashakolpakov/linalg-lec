\section{Guiding Question}

Which symmetric matrices behave like squared length?

\section{Planned Core Ideas}

\begin{itemize}
  \item quadratic forms have the shape $x^TAx$;
  \item $B^TB$ is positive semidefinite;
  \item Gram matrices and covariance matrices are PSD;
  \item trace records total diagonal mass and, spectrally, total variance;
  \item PSD matrices connect geometry, optimization, and data.
\end{itemize}

\section{Planned Python Thread}

Generate matrices of the form $B^TB$, inspect eigenvalues, and visualize small
quadratic forms.
