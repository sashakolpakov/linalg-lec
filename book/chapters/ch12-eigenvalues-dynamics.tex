\section{Guiding Question}

Which directions does a linear map preserve?

\section{Planned Core Ideas}

\begin{itemize}
  \item eigenvectors keep their direction under a linear map;
  \item eigenvalues give the scaling factor;
  \item eigenvalues detect non-invertibility of $A-\lambda I$;
  \item matrix powers are simple on eigenvectors;
  \item dynamics reveal dominant spectral behavior.
\end{itemize}

\section{Planned Python Thread}

Simulate $x_{t+1}=Ax_t$, compare symmetric and nonsymmetric examples, and use
\texttt{np.linalg.eig} or \texttt{np.linalg.eigh} carefully.
