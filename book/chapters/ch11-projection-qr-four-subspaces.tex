\section{Guiding Question}

How does orthogonality organize approximation and the structure of a matrix?

\section{Planned Core Ideas}

\begin{itemize}
  \item projection onto a subspace gives best approximation;
  \item least-squares residuals are orthogonal to the column space;
  \item Gram-Schmidt builds orthogonal bases;
  \item QR is a stable way to solve least-squares problems;
  \item the four fundamental subspaces reveal domain and codomain structure.
\end{itemize}

orthogonality on nearly dependent vectors.
