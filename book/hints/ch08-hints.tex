\section*{Chapter 8}
\paragraph{8.A1.} Perturb $\hat{x}$ in the direction of one coordinate vector,
or use the projection theorem for $\col(A)$.
\paragraph{8.A2.} One inclusion is immediate. For the other, multiply
$A^TAv=0$ on the left by $v^T$.
\paragraph{8.B1.} Use $\Proj_a(b)=\frac{\inner{b}{a}}{\inner{a}{a}}a$.
\paragraph{8.B2.} Compute $A^TA$ and $A^Tb$ first; the resulting system is
$2\times 2$.
\paragraph{8.C1.} The residual is \texttt{r = b - A @ x}; test
\texttt{A.T @ r}.
\paragraph{8.C2.} To avoid forming an inverse, compute
\texttt{H = A @ np.linalg.solve(A.T @ A, A.T)}.
\paragraph{8.D1.} Start with a line in $\R^2$, such as the span of
$(1,0)$, and choose $b$ with a nonzero perpendicular component.
