\section*{Chapter 3}

\paragraph{3.A3.}
Start with two vectors $x,y$ satisfying $Ax=0$ and $Ay=0$. Apply $A$ to
$\lambda x+\mu y$.

\paragraph{3.B1.}
First ask whether the zero vector belongs to the set. Then test closure under
addition.

\paragraph{3.C2.}
The sum of two unit vectors need not be a unit vector.

\paragraph{3.D1.}
For the union question, try the $x$-axis and $y$-axis in $\R^2$.
