\section*{Chapter 7}
\paragraph{7.A1.} Substitute the projection formula and simplify
$\inner{u-\Proj_v(u)}{v}$.
\paragraph{7.A2.} Expand both squared norms using the inner product and cancel
the cross terms.
\paragraph{7.B1.} Use $\Proj_u(v)=\frac{\inner{v}{u}}{\inner{u}{u}}u$.
\paragraph{7.B2.} Remember to weight the second coordinate by $4$ in every
inner product.
\paragraph{7.C1.} Divide the dot product by the product of the two norms.
\paragraph{7.C2.} Ignore any randomly generated zero vector, or regenerate it.
Floating-point error may give a value like $1+10^{-15}$.
\paragraph{7.D1.} Try the standard dot product and a weighted dot product.
Use vectors whose second coordinates are not both zero.
