\chapter*{Dependency Map}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Dependency Map}

The core path is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{vectors and spaces}
\longrightarrow
\text{maps and rank} \\
&\longrightarrow
\text{geometry, least squares, and coordinates}
\longrightarrow
\text{spectral structure} \\
&\longrightarrow
\text{SVD, PCA, and low rank}.
\end{aligned}
\]

For a short course, Chapters~15--17 can be optional. The determinant, tensor,
and exterior-algebra appendices are mathematical complements rather than
prerequisites for the main path.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\toprule
Lab & Primary chapters & Theme \\
\midrule
1 & 1--3 & vectors, vector spaces, subspaces, span \\
2 & 4--6 & linear maps, rank, and solving $Ax=b$ \\
3 & 7--9 & inner products, projection, least squares, QR \\

5 & 12--13 & PSD matrices, spectral theorem, Rayleigh quotients \\
6 & 14 & SVD, PCA, compression, and low-rank approximation \\
7 & 15 & image processing, convolution, and the DFT \\
8 & 16 & SVD denoising and matrix completion \\
9 & 17 & coding theory and the Hamming code project \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
