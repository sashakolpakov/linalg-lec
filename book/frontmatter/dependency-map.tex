\chapter*{Dependency Map}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Dependency Map}

The core path is:

\[
\text{vectors and spaces}
\longrightarrow
\text{maps and rank}
\longrightarrow
\text{geometry and least squares}
\longrightarrow
\text{spectral structure}
\longrightarrow
\text{SVD and low rank}.
\]

For a short course, Chapters 18 and 19 can be optional. The determinant,
tensor, and exterior-algebra appendices are mathematical complements rather
than prerequisites for the main path.
