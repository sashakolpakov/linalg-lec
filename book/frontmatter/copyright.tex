\chapter*{Copyright and Draft Status}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Copyright and Draft Status}

This is a working draft. The source is organized as a modular book separate
from the original lecture notes, labs, figures, and demos. The first goal is a
stable chapter architecture; prose, exercises, hints, and labs can then be
expanded chapter by chapter.

No semester-specific course date is used in the book source. Course offerings
can wrap this book with their own schedule, assignments, and selected labs.
