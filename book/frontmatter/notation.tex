\chapter*{Notation}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Notation}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Symbol & Meaning \\
\midrule
$\F$ & a field of scalars \\
$\R$ & the real numbers \\
$\C$ & the complex numbers \\
$\F^n$ & coordinate vectors of length $n$ over $\F$ \\
$\F^{m\times n}$ & $m$ by $n$ matrices over $\F$ \\
$V,W$ & vector spaces \\
$T:V\to W$ & a function, often a linear map \\
$A,B,X$ & matrices \\
$\Ker A$ & the kernel/null space of $A$ \\
$\Img A$ & the image/column space of $A$ \\
$\Rank A$ & the dimension of the image of $A$ \\
$\Span\{v_1,\ldots,v_k\}$ & all linear combinations of the listed vectors \\
$\inner{u}{v}$ & an inner product \\
$\norm{v}$ & a norm \\
$A^T$ & transpose of a real matrix \\
$U\Sigma V^T$ & singular value decomposition \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The book uses both ``image'' and ``column space'' when discussing matrices.
For a matrix $A$, these refer to the same subspace: all vectors of the form
$Ax$.
